# Mission

The mission of this project is to make cybersecurity and modern technology feel approachable for people who once thought it was too late to learn.

Many people feel left behind by Linux, networking, hardware, AI, internet traffic, and cybersecurity.

This guide is designed to slow those concepts down and explain them in a practical way.

The goal is confidence, not speed.

The goal is understanding, not memorization.

The goal is to help beginners learn a valuable skill in today’s digital world without pressure, confusion, or technical gatekeeping.
