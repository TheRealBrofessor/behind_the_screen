# OPSEC and Privacy

## Goal

The goal of this section is to help beginners understand how personal information can be exposed and how to reduce unnecessary risk.

## What You Will Learn

- What OPSEC means
- What a digital footprint is
- Why privacy settings matter
- Why public Wi-Fi can be risky
- Why two-factor authentication matters
- How social media can reveal information
- How to think before sharing online
- How to reduce avoidable exposure

## Beginner Explanation

OPSEC means operational security.

In simple terms, OPSEC is about protecting information that could be used against you.

A digital footprint is the trail of information you leave online.

This can include:

- Social media posts
- Photos
- Email addresses
- Usernames
- Location history
- Old accounts
- Public records
- Data breaches

Privacy does not mean hiding everything.

It means controlling what you expose and understanding the risks.

Good OPSEC starts with asking:

What information am I giving away?

Who can see it?

How could it be misused?

## Important Terms

OPSEC: A way of thinking about what information you expose.

Digital Footprint: The trail of information connected to you online.

Metadata: Extra information attached to files, photos, messages, or activity.

Two-Factor Authentication: A second layer of account protection.

Public Wi-Fi: A shared network that may be less trustworthy.

Data Breach: An incident where private information is exposed.

## Why This Matters

People often reveal more information than they realize.

Small pieces of information can combine into a bigger picture.

For example:

- A username can connect accounts.
- A photo can reveal location.
- A public post can reveal routine.
- A data breach can expose an email.
- A weak recovery email can expose other accounts.

OPSEC helps you slow down and think before sharing.

## Basic Activity

Search your own name or username online.

Write down:

1. What information appears?
2. Is any of it outdated?
3. Is any of it too personal?
4. Are there old accounts you forgot about?
5. What could you clean up or secure?
6. Do the same usernames appear across multiple sites?
7. Could someone guess personal details from public posts?

## Key Takeaway

OPSEC is not paranoia.

It is awareness.

The goal is to reduce unnecessary exposure and make smarter choices with personal information.
